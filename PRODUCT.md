# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating the author's Kotlin and Android work, looking for evidence of technical range and product taste.

## Product Purpose

Present independently built applications and libraries as distinct, credible products rather than a generic project list.

## Brand Personality

Personal, tactile, and technically precise.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS card grids, default developer-portfolio aesthetics, and decoration that obscures the product itself.

## Design Principles

- Let each product retain its own visual character.
- Make technical work immediately scannable.
- Prefer concrete interface cues over abstract decoration.
- Keep the surrounding portfolio calm enough for product work to stand out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain legible contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
